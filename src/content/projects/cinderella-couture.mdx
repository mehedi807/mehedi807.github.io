## The product

Cinderella Couture is an e-commerce platform for a fashion retailer. Its backend
connects product discovery, payments, order fulfillment, inventory, and shipping.

## What I worked on

- Django and Next.js commerce functionality with order fulfillment and inventory tracking.
- Semantic product search using pgvector embeddings for natural-language discovery.
- Square and PayPal payment integrations.
- Automated shipping-label generation through ShipStation.
